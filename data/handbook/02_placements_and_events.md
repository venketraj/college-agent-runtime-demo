## 5. Placement eligibility

- Students are eligible for campus placement drives from the **7th semester**.
- Minimum criteria: **CGPA of 7.0 or above**, **no standing arrears**, and **overall attendance of 80% or above** in the previous semester.
- Students who accept an offer from a **"Dream" company (CTC ≥ 10 LPA)** cannot sit for further drives. Students with a regular offer may attempt **one more Dream company**.
- Placement training runs every **Saturday from 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM** in the Seminar Hall, Block C, from the 5th semester onwards. Attendance in training is mandatory for placement registration.

## 6. AI & Robotics Club ("NeuralNest")

- NeuralNest meets every **Wednesday at 4:15 PM in Lab 304**.
- Membership is free; register through the club coordinator, **Dr. K. Meenakshi**.
- Every member must complete **one mini-project per semester** and present it at the club demo day.
- The club gives free access to **two GPU workstations** for project work, bookable through the club portal in **2-hour slots**.

## 7. Upcoming events

- **TechVerse 2026** — national-level technical symposium, **14–15 November 2026**. Events: paper presentation, 24-hour hackathon, AI quiz, and a "Build with LLMs" workshop. Registration fee: **Rs. 300 per student**, last date **5 November 2026**.

## 8. Library

- Students can borrow up to **4 books for 14 days**. The late fine is **Rs. 2 per day per book**.
- The digital library (IEEE, Springer, NPTEL) is accessible on campus Wi-Fi and through the remote-access portal.
- The library is open **8:00 AM – 8:00 PM** on weekdays and **9:00 AM – 2:00 PM** on Saturdays.

## 9. Labs

- Programming labs (Labs 301–306) are open for self-practice from **4:00 PM to 6:30 PM** on weekdays.
- Food and drinks are not allowed in labs. Personal laptops may be connected only to the **"VT-Student"** Wi-Fi network.
