# Department of CSE — Student Handbook 2026-27 (fictional demo data)

> This handbook is sample data created for a classroom AI demo. It is not an official college document.

## 1. Attendance policy

- Every student must maintain a minimum of **75% attendance in each subject** to be eligible to write the end-semester examination.
- Students with attendance between **65% and 74%** may apply for **condonation** with a valid medical certificate. The condonation fee is **Rs. 750 per subject**, paid at the accounts office within 5 working days of the notice.
- Students below **65%** in any subject are **not eligible** for condonation and must re-register for the subject in the next semester.
- Attendance is updated on the student portal every **Friday by 5:00 PM**.
- On-duty (OD) leave for approved hackathons, symposiums and sports events is counted as present, up to a maximum of **10 OD days per semester**. OD requests must be approved by the class mentor **at least 2 days before** the event.

## 2. Mentor system

- Every student is assigned a **faculty mentor** for all four years. One mentor guides about 10–12 students.
- If a student's attendance falls **below 75% in any subject**, the mentor must email the student and their parent within **3 working days**, and schedule a one-to-one meeting.
- Mentors hold a group meeting with their mentees on the **first Monday of every month** at 3:30 PM.

## 3. Internal assessment

- Each theory subject has **two Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT-1 and CAT-2)** of 50 marks each, and one assignment of 10 marks.
- Internal marks are out of **40**; the end-semester exam is out of **60**.
- A student who misses a CAT for a medical reason may write a **retest within 7 days** with mentor approval. Only one retest is allowed per subject per semester.

## 4. Grading

- The minimum passing grade is **"C" (50 marks)** combined internal + external.
- CGPA is calculated on a 10-point scale. Students with **CGPA ≥ 8.5 and no arrears** are listed on the **Dean's List** each semester.
